# Homepage reshoot, 7 images

The first homepage set drifted across four continents because only image 001
named a region. These seven replace 002 through 008. Image 001 is correct and
stays as it is.

**Keep the same numbering.** Save as `002.jpg` through `008.jpg`, or use the exact
filename shown. `scripts/place-images.mjs` overwrites the existing files in place,
so nothing else needs touching.

## Treatment A, location and documentary

Append to every prompt below.

> Desaturated documentary photography, natural available light, muted palette of
> concrete grey, sand, weathered steel and off-white. Exactly one saturated red
> object present naturally in the frame, kept small and off-centre. Wide, calm,
> static composition with generous empty space on one side. Medium depth of field.
> Photorealistic. No text, no lettering, no signage copy, no logos, no watermarks.
> No faces looking at camera.

## The two rules that matter

**Red is an object, never a filter.** One genuinely red thing in an otherwise
muted frame. The first set got this right every time; keep it.

**Region is not optional.** Every prompt below states Saudi Arabia and lists what
it must not look like. The exclusions are load-bearing, not decoration.

### 002 · `home/pillar-loyalty.jpg` · 16:9 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** A neighbourhood bakery counter in a Gulf city, hands passing a
paper bag of fresh Arabic bread across to a regular customer, both figures cropped
at the shoulders, one wearing a white thobe cuff. Warm interior, worn counter.
Red accent: the server's apron tie.
Must not read as European, North American or East Asian.

### 003 · `home/pillar-sustainability.jpg` · 16:9 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** Flattened cardboard stacked neatly beside sorted recycling
crates in a clean service lane behind a row of shops in a Gulf city, sand-coloured
rendered walls, early morning light, a palm visible. Orderly and cared-for, with a
worker at distance so it is not deserted. Red accent: one plastic crate.
No brick, no fire escapes, no dumpsters. Must not read as North American.

### 004 · `home/audience-consumers.jpg` · 3:4 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** Vertical. A shopper walking away from camera down a covered souq
arcade in a Gulf city, phone in hand at their side, slight motion blur, arches and
warm stone receding into depth. Red accent: a shopfront shutter far down the run.
No Victorian cast iron, no glass-and-steel arcade roof. Must not read as European.

### 005 · `home/audience-businesses.jpg` · 3:4 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** Vertical. A Gulf shopkeeper in a white thobe behind the counter
of a small hardware and household goods shop, seen from the side, reviewing a
tablet. Stocked shelves rise behind them. Calm, competent, unglamorous.
Red accent: a product box on a shelf. Must not read as a European workshop.

### 006 · `home/audience-communities.jpg` · 3:4 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** Vertical. A street of small independent storefronts in a Gulf

people in Gulf dress at middle distance, none prominent. Awnings and shopfront
signage rendered soft and out of focus so no lettering is legible.
Red accent: one awning. No Japanese, Latin or Cyrillic signage.

### 007 · `home/audience-sustainability.jpg` · 3:4 · Treatment A
**Saudi Arabia.** Vertical. Hands placing a reusable steel container into a stacked
crate at a Gulf produce market, dates and local produce on the stall behind, warm

No cold-weather clothing, no European market awnings.

### 008 · `home/app.jpg` · 3:4 · Treatment A
